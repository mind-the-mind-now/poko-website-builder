<b>philosopher</b>

::: flow

Specialist in the political, ecological, and metaphysical aspects of sustainability

:::

***

Honorary Professor, University of Lausanne. Visiting Professor, EDDEC-University of Montreal (2017); Mercier Chair, Catholic University of Louvain (2023-2024); Royal Academy of Morocco (2023- ). Series Editor at PUF (Presses Universitaires de France). Officer of the Legion of Honour and the National Order of Merit. Winner of the "Solitary Walker" Prize (2003), the Veolia Environment Prize (2015), and the Montyon Prize from the French Academy (2025). Recent publications: Devastation: The Question of Evil Today, PUF, 2024; Lessons from Planetary Boundaries, Actes Sud, 2025; with Ernst Zuercher, *Rights of Nature vs. Environmental Rights*, Frémeaux Associés, 2026; *Reinventing Progress*, Utopia, 2027.
